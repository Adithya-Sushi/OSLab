Closest actuator characterization
----------------------------------
Abhiyan Dhakal and Sanjog Sigdel, A Bounded Reclaim Actuator for PSI-Guided
Compressed Memory: A Controlled Ablation, arXiv:2608.13689v1 (13 August 2026).
https://arxiv.org/html/2608.13689v1

Its one-time request, compressed-swap setting and compute/SQLite canaries overlap
the measurement ingredients. The candidate donor estimator must establish its
own distinction and operational value. No novelty follows from a Linux VM,
bounded request size, checksum, or compression-dependent outcome alone.
